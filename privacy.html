Privacy Policy

GlobalRisk is a real-time global monitoring platform that aggregates publicly available information about conflicts, disasters, severe weather, aviation, and other significant events. This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, and protect information when you visit the site.

1. Information we may collect

We may collect limited technical information such as browser type, device type, operating system, page views, referring pages, time spent on the site, and aggregate usage data. We may also rely on standard browser cookies, local storage, and analytics tools to understand traffic and improve performance.

2. Cookies and analytics

We may use analytics, advertising technology, and cookies to measure traffic, monitor site health, understand usage patterns, and support the service. This data is generally aggregated and used to improve reliability, user experience, and the relevance of any advertising that may appear on the platform.

3. Public data and third-party sources

GlobalRisk aggregates public information from third-party news sites, government feeds, scientific and emergency-management sources, flight trackers, and other public data providers. We do not control the privacy practices of those external sources, and their own policies apply to the data they provide.

4. How we use information

We use information to maintain the service, improve map and feed performance, detect outages or bad data, understand trends in usage, and provide relevant advertising or site features when applicable. We do not sell personal data.

5. Advertising and partner services

Where advertising or analytics services are enabled, they may use cookies, device identifiers, and usage data to serve personalized or contextual ads in accordance with their own terms and applicable privacy laws. You may be able to adjust browser settings or ad preferences to limit tracking in supported systems.

6. Data retention

We keep operational and analytics data only as long as necessary to support the service, maintain security, and comply with lawful obligations. Some logs or aggregated data may be retained longer for troubleshooting, performance monitoring, or compliance purposes.

7. Your choices

You may disable cookies in your browser or use privacy tools that limit tracking. Note that some site functions may be affected if cookies or local storage are blocked.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact hello@globalrisk.site.
