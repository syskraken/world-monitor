About GlobalRisk

GlobalRisk is a real-time global risk monitoring platform that aggregates and visualizes publicly available information about conflicts, natural disasters, severe weather, aviation activity and other significant events.

Data Sources

GlobalRisk draws on public news feeds, official emergency-management and geological sources, satellite and fire detection systems, aviation tracking data, and other reputable public data providers. Examples include Google News, USGS earthquake feeds, NASA EONET, GDACS, NOAA, and public aviation trackers where available.

Methodology

Events are collected from multiple sources, normalized into a common schema, categorized by type and severity, filtered for relevance, and displayed on the map and feed in near real time. Source quality, reporting lag, and regional coverage can vary by feed and by event.

Important disclaimer

This dashboard is for informational and situational-awareness purposes only. Data may be delayed, incomplete, or occasionally inaccurate. It is not an official emergency warning system and should not replace local authorities, official alerts, or professional guidance.
